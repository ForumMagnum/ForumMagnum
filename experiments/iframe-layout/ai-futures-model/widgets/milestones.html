Five milestones
  1. AC
    Automated Coder
    A lab would sooner lose all of its human engineers than its AI coders.
  2. SAR
    Superhuman AI Researcher
    Better research taste than the best human researcher.
  3. SIAR
    Superintelligent AI Researcher
    Twice as far above SAR as the best human is above the median one.
  4. TED
    Top-Expert-Dominating AI
    Does roughly 95% of remote work at the level of the best experts.
  5. ASI
    Superintelligence
    Two more such gaps above TED-AI, at every cognitive task.
"Timelines" means the wait until AC. "Takeoff" means AC to ASI.
