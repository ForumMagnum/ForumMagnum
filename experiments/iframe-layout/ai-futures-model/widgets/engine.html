The engine: one loop, one brake
  1. Compute and coders, human and AI
  2. Experiments, how many you can run
  3. Research effort, times research taste
  4. Software efficiency, where ideas get harder to find
  5. Effective compute, times training compute
  6. AI capability, better coders and better taste, which feeds back to the start
Progress flows around the loop and comes back as better AI researchers. The dots bunch up at the brake: every doubling of software efficiency costs more research than the last. Whether the loop or the brake wins decides how fast things go.
